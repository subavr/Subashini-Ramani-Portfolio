How I work

Four principles behind the results.

The way I approach quality, escalations, teams and products.

Get it right the first time

I build quality in through process, frameworks, tools and methods, so the team is not firefighting at the last minute.

Stay calm in escalations

I look at the data across the full product lifecycle, find where the miss happened, and take corrective action at every stage of the cycle.

Lead with openness

I am approachable and communicate a lot. I explain my ideas and opinions in detail, ask for my team’s ideas, and encourage them to bring their own.

Build for the customer

I start with market research and look for what is missing against customer expectations, then design the architecture and solution, always thinking about how the customer will use it.
